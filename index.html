01

Security Clearance Polygraph

How well does the spy know his asset?
7 min
60% Funny 40% Savage 📸 Photo Op

CONCEPT

The groom sits in a chair facing the door (the "polygraph chair"). Ji Mui reads questions about Trish. Wrong answers = punishment for the ENTIRE squad. The groomsmen learn quickly that the groom's ignorance costs everyone.

How to Run It

  1. Place a chair in the hallway — this is the "polygraph station." Groom must sit. No phone, no lifelines.
  2. Ji Mui reads 8-10 questions about Trish (prepared in advance with Trish's answers sealed in envelopes).
  3. Easy warmups: Trish's birthday, her favourite food, the date they first met.
  4. Trap questions: Her shoe size, her most-used emoji, the first movie they watched together, her childhood pet's name, her order at her favourite restaurant.
  5. Wrong answer punishment: Groom + all 13 groomsmen do 10 push-ups per wrong answer (on the floor right there). OR they must yell "TRISH IS ALWAYS RIGHT" at the top of their lungs facing the beach.
  6. After 3+ wrong answers, start adding "penalty interest" — additional push-ups stack (10, then 15, then 20…)
  7. Twist: One question should be "What would Trish say is your most annoying habit?" — both answers are read aloud. Comedy gold.
🎬
Photo moment: Get the groom sweating in the polygraph chair. Film the groomsmen's faces when they realize wrong answers punish THEM.
📦
Supplies: Question cards, sealed answer envelopes, a chair, optional desk lamp for "interrogation lighting"
👤
Run by: Ji Mui 2 (the serious one — play it deadpan for maximum comedy)
02

Field Agent Fitness Exam

A spy must be in peak physical condition
7 min
50% Funny 50% Savage 📸 Photo Op

CONCEPT

Move outside to the beach/pool deck. The 13 groomsmen split into 2 teams for a relay. Losers get extra punishment. This is where the beach setting really shines.

How to Run It

  1. Warm-up: Everyone (including the groom) holds a plank. Last man standing earns a "free pass" token good for one future station. The groom must last longer than at least 5 others or everyone restarts.
  2. Beach Relay — "Agent Extraction Drill": Split into 2 teams of ~6 (groom participates on both, running twice).
  3. Relay legs:
    • Leg 1: Crab walk 10m on the sand
    • Leg 2: Carry a teammate piggyback 10m
    • Leg 3: Army crawl under a string/towel line 5m
    • Leg 4: Spin around a bat 10 times then sprint 10m straight (hilarious to watch)
  4. Losing team: 30-second wall-sit against the building while singing a love song chosen by the ji muis (suggest something embarrassing like a Backstreet Boys ballad).
  5. Groom penalty (always): Whether his team wins or loses, the groom must do 5 burpees "for love."
🎬
Photo moment: The bat-spin-then-sprint is ALWAYS hilarious — video this. Also the piggyback carries on sand.
📦
Supplies: A towel or string for the crawl line, a bat/stick for spinning, timer
👤
Run by: Evelyn (lead) — you're the drill sergeant here
03

Interrogation Room

Suan Tian Ku La — The four flavours of marriage
7 min
70% Savage 30% Funny 📸 Photo Op

CONCEPT

The classic Suan Tian Ku La (酸甜苦辣 — sour, sweet, bitter, spicy) with a CIA twist. Each flavour represents a "truth serum" the groom and his team must consume during interrogation.

How to Run It

  1. Prepare 4 "truth serums" (drinks/bites):
    • SOUR (酸): Pure lime/calamansi juice, no sugar. Full shot glass.
    • SWEET (甜): Condensed milk mixed with soy sauce (looks sweet, tastes confusing).
    • BITTER (苦): Bitter melon juice or strong unsweetened coffee + wasabi shot.
    • SPICY (辣): Hot sauce (bring Samyang or local chili paste) on a cracker. This is the boss level.
  2. The groom goes first and must consume ALL FOUR while reciting why he loves Trish (one reason per flavour). If he pauses, stutters, or makes a face — restart that flavour.
  3. Then 4 volunteers (or victims chosen by Ji Muis) from the groomsmen must each take one flavour. They must toast "To Trish and [Groom]!" while consuming it.
  4. Savage twist: If the groom's love declarations aren't sincere enough, the Ji Muis vote to make him do a bonus round — spicy + bitter combined. The "Double Agent Cocktail."
  5. Water is available but costs 1 push-up per sip. (This is important — don't be monsters, just expensive ones.)
🎬
Photo moment: Close-up reactions on the spicy round. The groom trying to say sweet things while dying inside. Pure gold.
📦
Supplies: Limes/calamansi, condensed milk, soy sauce, bitter melon juice or strong coffee, wasabi, hot sauce (Samyang level), crackers, shot glasses, cups, water bottles, napkins
👤
Run by: Ji Mui 3 — deliver each "truth serum" with a straight face and a dossier label
04

Code Breaking

Decrypt the intel to proceed
7 min
80% Funny 20% Sweet

CONCEPT

The team must solve puzzles and decode messages about the couple's relationship. Brain over brawn — but there's a catch. Time pressure + physical penalties for wrong guesses keep it chaotic.

How to Run It

  1. Puzzle 1 — Photo Decode: Show 5 zoomed-in/cropped photos of Trish (her eye, her hand with a specific ring, a childhood photo, a favourite place, her handwriting). Team must identify what each photo shows. 30 seconds per photo.
  2. Puzzle 2 — Cipher Message: Give the groom a coded love message from Trish written in a simple cipher (A=1, B=2 or reversed alphabet). He has 2 minutes to decode it. The message should be something sweet and personal — maybe the first text she ever sent him, or an inside joke.
  3. Puzzle 3 — Emoji Story: Display a sequence of emojis that tells the story of the couple's relationship (first meeting → first date → proposal, etc.). The team must narrate what each emoji represents. Every wrong guess = one person has to serenade Trish's door with "My Heart Will Go On" for 10 seconds.
  4. Bonus — "Agent's Oath": The team must collectively write a 4-line poem/pledge declaring why the groom deserves Trish. Read it aloud. Ji Muis rate it 1-10. Below a 7? Rewrite. This is secretly sweet but publicly humiliating — perfect.
🎬
Photo moment: 13 grown men crowded around a cipher trying to crack it. The serenade penalty. The poem reading.
📦
Supplies: Printed cropped photos, cipher message (prepare with Trish), emoji sequence card, paper + pens for the poem, timer
👤
Run by: Ji Mui 4 — keep the timer strict, hype the pressure

Disguise & Infiltration

A good spy can become anyone
7 min
90% Funny 10% Savage 📸 Best Photo Op

CONCEPT

The team's most photogenic station. Groomsmen must "disguise" themselves to infiltrate the bridal suite. This is where the 60/40 funny-to-savage ratio lives its best life.

How to Run It

  1. Round 1 — "Undercover as the Bride": Pick 3 groomsmen. Each gets 90 seconds to "become Trish" using a supply box of props: a wig or towel as hair, lipstick, clip-on earrings, a sarong as a dress. They must pose and give their best "Trish impression." Ji Muis + Trish (watching from inside) vote on the best one.
  2. Round 2 — The Groom's Makeover: The groom sits still while the groomsmen (NOT the Ji Muis) apply makeup on him — lipstick, blush, eyeliner. Timer: 2 minutes. He cannot look in a mirror until the big reveal. This photo goes in the wedding slideshow.
  3. Round 3 — TikTok Dance Challenge: The entire squad must learn a 30-second choreographed dance (pick a trending one or a classic like "Nobody" by Wonder Girls for the Singaporean crowd). They perform it together outside Trish's door. No half-efforts — Ji Muis judge and can make them redo it.
  4. Groom penalty: Regardless of results, the groom must do the dance SOLO one more time while yelling "I LOVE YOU TRISH" at the bridge of the song.
🎬
Photo moment: THIS IS THE STATION. Every single round is Instagram/TikTok gold. Film everything. The groom's makeover reveal is the centrepiece — get his reaction in the mirror.

06

Final Debrief — The Extraction

Mission complete. Almost.
7 min
80% Sweet 20% Funny 📸 The Money Shot

CONCEPT

The emotional closer. After all the chaos, this is the moment that makes people cry. The groom delivers his "final debrief" — a heartfelt declaration to Trish through the closed door. Then the ang bao negotiation. Then the door opens.

How to Run It

  1. The Groom's Declaration: Standing at the door, the groom must say (not read from a phone!) 3 promises to Trish for their marriage. These must be specific and personal, not generic. Ji Muis reject anything like "I'll love you forever" — too easy. Make him dig deep.
  2. The Brothers' Testimony: Pick 2-3 groomsmen (Edmund, Ron, and one childhood friend like Kenley work well — different eras of friendship). Each says ONE sentence about why the groom deserves Trish. Keep it tight — this isn't a roast, it's a testimony.
  3. The Squad Shout: On the count of 3, all 13 groomsmen yell something the groom has prepared — a line in Trish's love language, a meaningful phrase from their relationship, or simply "TRISH, OPEN THE DOOR, YOUR SPY HAS COME HOME!" 🕵️
  4. Ang Bao negotiation (see next section) happens now — don't let this drag.
  5. THE DOOR OPENS. Music cue: James Bond theme or something triumphant. Groom walks in. First look. Everyone cheers. Mission complete. 🎉
🎬
Photo moment: Film the groom's face when the door opens. Film Trish's reaction. This is THE shot. Have two cameras — one on each side of the door.
👤
Run by: Evelyn (lead) — you control the pacing here. Don't rush the emotional beats.
Section 04

Ang Bao Strategy

🧧 The Red Packet Negotiation

This happens at the very end, after Station 6. It's the "ransom" for Trish's release.

  1. Start high, negotiate down. Open with an absurd number (like $9,999) — it's theatre, everyone knows it. The groom will counter. Settle on an auspicious amount with 8s ($888, $388, $168).
  2. Accept red packets only — make them bring actual ang baos. No Venmo, no PayPal. This is tradition.
  3. The comedy beats: Slide a receipt under the door: "INVOICE: Emotional Damages — $X." If the first offer is too low, slide it back with "REJECTED" stamped on it. Escalate theatrically.
  4. The backup: If negotiation stalls, add a final challenge — "sing us one more song and we'll settle for $[lower amount]."
  5. Keep it to 2-3 minutes max. The emotional high from Station 6 is the real payload — don't kill it with a long haggle.
